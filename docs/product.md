# Product

## Name

Manabi Voice

## Concept

Manabi Voice is a web app for learning by speaking.

Users speak freely about what they learned after class, reading, work, or daily discoveries.

The app does not simply summarize. It checks whether the user's explanation is clear, sufficient, and factually safe.

## Core value

The app helps users notice gaps in their understanding by checking:

- what they actually said
- what claims they made
- what was vague but acceptable
- what was too vague to confirm understanding
- what explanations were insufficient
- what should be fact checked
- what question the user should answer next
